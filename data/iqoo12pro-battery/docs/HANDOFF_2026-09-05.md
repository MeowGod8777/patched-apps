Installed artifact:

```text

```

Current verified semantics:

- private DB: `power8`
- table: `records`
- History timestamp = session start (`beginTime`)
- current sample interval = 3 s
- History first duration = valid screen-on sample count × 3 s
- History second duration = whole-session wall span (`endTime - beginTime`)
- History average power = `avg(current * voltage)` over valid screen-on discharge/not-charging samples
- theoretical runtime ~= `batteryEnergy / abs(avgPower) × 0.9`
- nominal energy helper voltage = 3.86 V, with documented conditional doubled-energy branch
- History `x.xh` formatter truncates downward in 0.1 h / 6 min buckets
- per-app rows group by package + Scene mode; app duration = valid sample count × 3 s

The current-page capacity/status/voltage/temperature header is live GlobalStatus and must never be attached to an old History session.

## Screenshot production contract

Routine user behavior:

1. periodically capture recent Scene History, preferably with one overlap anchor
2. preserve timestamp + both History duration values
3. capture detail for new finalized sessions when exact screen-on, average power, theoretical runtime, or granular app attribution is needed
4. current/live screenshots remain provisional until matched to finalized History

Assistant behavior:

- parse
- apply History precision bounds
- dedupe using timestamp + power + durations + date/curve/app evidence, never watts alone
- append without rewriting old evidence
- retain granular future app rows when available
- reconcile old live captures when later History identity appears
- update rolling statistics after enough new sessions accumulate

No routine OCR, CLEAR_TASK/force-stop automation, background Runner loop, or private-DB probing.

### Coverage warning discovered by natural-use screenshots

The uploaded v2.5 raw batch covers `2026-09-05T22:52:49.910+08:00` through `2026-09-08T11:03:47.107+08:00`, but screenshots show that short/active Wi-Fi periods can be missing from the event timeline:

- `2026-09-06 08:03:16` visibly shows Wi-Fi while the surrounding context reports/retains `offline`.
- `2026-09-07 03:49:21` visibly shows Wi-Fi during a span with no corresponding Wi-Fi transition.
- `2026-09-08 11:25:01` is consistent with `home_wifi / DaFengLi_5G` at the capture instant.

This may be missed transition/background observation rather than a deterministic classifier error, but it is enough to invalidate blind session-wide joining.

Therefore:

- raw v2.5 data remains preserved in `generated/2026-09-08/context_events_v2_uploaded_raw.csv`
- do not bulk-promote the raw batch into canonical context without qualification
- do not label long sessions by wall-time dominance of `offline` vs Wi-Fi
- Scene average power is screen-on-only; current MacroDroid events are wall-clock context and are not screen-on aligned
- use `network=unknown` when active-time transport cannot be established reliably
